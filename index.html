The Revenue Operating System Sprint

A focused engagement to find what's slowing growth — and a plan to fix it.

The sprint connects strategy to execution across five layers: people and org design, customer and revenue processes, data and decision-making, technology and automation, and cross-functional ownership. The goal isn't another strategy deck. It's a shared diagnosis, clear choices, and a plan your team can actually run.

What you get
01

A clear diagnosis of what's breaking

I review your strategy, metrics, org, customer journey, workflows, and systems — then name the few problems that matter most: where revenue is lost or delayed, where ownership is unclear, and where tools add complexity instead of leverage.

02

A shared revenue operating model

One model for how the commercial org should work together: the outcomes you're after, the segments and motions that deserve priority, who owns each part of the lifecycle, and the decisions, systems, and rhythms that support execution.

03

A prioritized execution roadmap

A practical plan that separates what to fix now, what to test before scaling, what to stop doing, and what needs deeper change — with an owner and a measure for each, sequenced by impact, effort, and readiness.

04

A simple decision & learning system

A lightweight way to capture the problem, the decision, the assumptions, and what actually happened — so hard-won knowledge compounds instead of disappearing into Slack threads and people's heads.

The idea behind the work

Align. Integrate. Collaborate.

Winning revenue organizations do three things well.

Align

Agree on the goals, customer priorities, measures, and choices that guide the business.

Integrate

Connect the roles, workflows, data, and systems needed to execute those choices across the lifecycle.

Collaborate

Build the rhythms and shared accountability that let teams solve problems and act as one.

Together these create two loops: execution loops that turn strategy into action, and learning loops that capture what worked and why — so the organization compounds its know-how over time.

Why alignment and learning matter

Two loops decide whether you grow.

When the parts of your revenue engine connect — and when you actually learn from results — growth compounds. When they don't, the same problems repeat.
